Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

JWALA is a food product. For hygiene and safety, we cannot accept returns of opened or delivered bottles. We will always make things right if your order arrives damaged or wrong.

Cancellations

  • You can cancel an order for a full refund any time before it is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp or email thehotrodbbq@gmail.com with your order details.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

Damaged, leaking or wrong items

  • Send us a photo of the parcel and the product on WhatsApp (+91 77603 48176) or email within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Once we confirm the issue, we will send a free replacement or give you a full refund for the affected items, as you prefer.

How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are made to your original payment method through Razorpay.
  • Refunds are initiated within 2 business days of approval. Your bank may take 5–7 business days to show the amount.
