Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

We ship JWALA to addresses across India through courier partners booked via Shiprocket.

Shipping charges

  • ₹79 flat shipping on every order, anywhere in India.
  • Free shipping on the 6-Pack.
  • Shipping charges are shown before you pay.

Dispatch and delivery

  • We dispatch orders within 24 hours of payment confirmation, excluding Sundays and public holidays.
  • Delivery time depends on your pincode and the courier. Most orders arrive within 2–7 business days of dispatch; metro cities are usually faster.
  • As soon as your order ships, you receive a tracking link by SMS and email.

Address and delivery issues

  • Please enter a complete address with the correct pincode and a reachable phone number.
  • If the courier cannot deliver after repeated attempts, or the address is wrong, the parcel returns to us. We will reship once you pay the shipping charge again.
  • All orders are prepaid online. We don't offer cash on delivery.
